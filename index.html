How Bridge works

Three steps. No account required to start looking.

01

Search or filter

Tell us your age, budget, and interests, or just search plain language.

02

Check you qualify

Every card shows eligibility, cost, deadline, and location up front. No surprises after you apply.

03

Apply with confidence

Built in

Built for who gets left out

Bridge exists because access to opportunity shouldn't depend on who you know.

Multiple languages

Not every student is comfortable reading English forms. Bridge translates and simplifies on request.

Accessible by default

Screen reader support, keyboard navigation, and high-contrast text, not bolted on after launch.

Cost, always up front

Application fees, travel, and financial aid are shown before you click apply, never buried.

No assumptions

We don't assume reliable transportation, a stable home, or a parent who's done this before.
